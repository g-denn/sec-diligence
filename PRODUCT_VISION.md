# Product vision

## Purpose

Make the first pass of US small and micro-cap filing diligence faster to verify, not faster to speculate.

## User

An investor, analyst, journalist, or builder who needs a disciplined evidence ledger from SEC filings before deciding whether to do more independent work.

## In scope

English-language US SEC filings; identity resolution; dated primary-source citations; explicit facts, inferences, unknowns, and reproducible calculations.

## Out of scope

Security selection, stock picks, price targets, valuation, portfolio construction, trading, screeners, real-time market feeds, or personalized advice.

## Decision principles

Primary sources beat summaries. Unknown is not zero. A citation must let another reader reproduce the claim. A useful warning is specific and dated, not sensational.
