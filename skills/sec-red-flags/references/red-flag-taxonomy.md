# Red-flag taxonomy

Use this as a review checklist, not as a scoring model. Only include an item in the ledger when there is dated evidence or a material, clearly bounded unknown.

## Liquidity and going concern

Review going-concern language, cash and restricted cash, operating cash use, contractual obligations, debt maturities, covenant breaches, and management's stated funding plans. A cash runway calculation needs a defined period, units, formula, and cited inputs; do not call it a forecast.

## Capital structure and dilution

Review shares outstanding, authorized shares, preferred securities, warrants, convertibles, equity lines, ATM programs, reverse splits, beneficial-ownership limits, and subsequent financing. Separate issued shares from potential shares. Describe terms rather than predicting an outcome.

## Related parties and control

Review related-party notes, loans, management arrangements, customer/vendor relationships, voting control, director independence, material weaknesses, auditor changes, restatements, and disclosure controls. A relationship alone is not misconduct.

## Operating dependence and obligations

Review customer and supplier concentration, license dependence, contingent consideration, litigation, tax claims, commitments, off-balance-sheet arrangements, segment concentration, and material regulatory matters.

## Filing integrity

Review late filings, amended reports, auditor qualifications, non-reliance notices, internal-control conclusions, and unexplained changes in key disclosures. Do not make a fraud or scam accusation without quoting an official finding.
